## Brand commitments
The user rejected the oversized-wordmark/staggered-feed redesign and explicitly selected a cinematic, immersive direction. Photography must dominate, with dramatic framing and transitions. The identity is experimental editorial × cinematic architecture, in charcoal #171817, near black #101110, muted brass #B69A62, warm ivory #E9E4D9 and supporting grey #85847D. Preserve the original transparent silver/gold logo. Establish a recognisable identity through typography and composition even without the logo. Avoid identical project cards, generic luxury serif styling, ornamental gradients, fake loaders, custom cursors, liquid effects and excessive animation. Respect keyboard, touch and reduced motion.

## Functionality
A static identity-led homepage, studio introduction, five editorial project galleries, construction imagery and direct phone/Instagram contact. The opening uses three native scrolling photographic scenes: Threshold (Entrance), Room (Restaurant 1), Detail (Residence). No giant replacement wordmark. Five native expandable project rows reveal photographic spreads. Project photographs open in a keyboard-accessible full-frame viewer, with original-image links as a no-JavaScript fallback. Native disclosures hold construction images and the optional local brief download. The download sends no information; without JavaScript, contact links, navigation and photographs remain available and the unusable download form stays hidden. No backend, external runtime library, analytics or tracking.
