# Website redesign
Mode: Experience. Audience: prospective clients discovering HN Architects through Instagram. Existing photographs and the supplied phone and Instagram are the evidence; no locations, invented credentials, drawings or statistics.

## Direction contract
THESIS: An open architectural section: oversized condensed lettering counterbalances a tall photographic aperture. Establish the studio before revealing the portfolio. Reject the thumbnail-first index and the luxury-serif template.
OWN-WORLD: #171817 charcoal, #101110 deep ground, #E9E4D9 ivory, #B69A62 brass and #85847D supporting grey. Barlow Condensed Light display, Instrument body, small monospace photograph indices. Square frames, open corners, no cards or image filters.
STORY: Identity, studio, five curated projects, real construction evidence, direct conversation. All 38 original web photographs remain; galleries have independent editorial rhythms.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Small original logo and quiet navigation above a huge HN at left; a vertical Entrance photograph at right; ARCHITECTS spans the lower composition. The perspective selector changes the real doorway view; a studio anchor continues the story. Mobile brings HN beside the narrow aperture with the wordmark below, keeping the identity rather than stacking a desktop hero.
FORM: Code-led, user-pinned experimental editorial / cinematic architecture direction. Concept-seed attempted; the cloud skill launcher is absent (exit 127), so no seed or catalog assignment was produced. User explicitly delegates all art-direction choices and requests implementation without minor approval rounds.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: manually choose one of three actual Entrance perspectives inside the opening aperture. A shared open-corner focus treatment links image navigation and photographic study. Motion: one subtle, non-blocking aperture opening; otherwise direct response to interaction, disabled for reduced motion. No external runtime dependencies.
