# Design system

## Overview
A photographic monograph of actual work. The unchanged logo anchors a quiet, persistent header; the photographs carry identity and variety. Instrument Sans replaces the former repeated small-copy treatment. Italiana is reserved for project names and the enquiry heading, with no recurring italic slogans.

## Colors
Charcoal is the canvas. Warm paper and quiet stone separate reading roles. Gold is reserved for the existing logo, cursor, focus and restrained link feedback. Photographs retain their original character; there is no permanent color wash or saturation filter.

## Typography
Body and interface text use locally bundled Instrument Sans. Reading text is 16–17px; project captions are 18–19px. Use actual project names rather than promotional headings. The liquid title is an additional visual treatment, never the only accessible project label.

## Layout
The index begins with a large photograph and opens into an unequal, twelve-column project sequence. Residence spans the full width; subsequent projects alternate wider scenes with narrower vertical views. At 650px all projects stack in reading order and retain visible names. The studio and enquiries are compact factual passages, without a services-card grid or process panels. Each project has a separate static URL. Project galleries preserve entire photographic frames and include the supplied construction photographs in native disclosures. The address appears once, in contact information.

## Elevation & Depth
Photography, scale and open space provide depth. No cards, shadows, floating panels or decorative button compartments. A local bottom scrim supports hover-title contrast only.

## Shapes
Square photographic frames and thin horizontal rules. The custom cursor is the confirmed 5px gold dot, concentric 30px ring, expanding to 42px over controls.

## Components
Navigation is plain text: Projects, Studio, Contact. Actions have explicit labels with underline feedback and no decorative arrows. Project links include always-readable captions; mouse hover and keyboard focus trigger a short liquid displacement that settles into the unchanged project name. Reduced motion removes displacement and scaling. Touch screens use the persistent caption. Native construction disclosures and project-brief forms preserve semantics and visible gold focus rings.

## Do's and Don'ts
- Use only the supplied project photographs and original logo.
- Keep neutral project names exactly as confirmed by the user.
- Use restrained factual copy; do not invent location, date or project statistics.
- Preserve full photographic frames inside individual galleries.
- Do not restore stock moodboards, repeated slogans, category rows, arrow ornaments, or three-part meeting/process cards.
- Keep motion brief and interruptible; no looping liquid-text animation.
